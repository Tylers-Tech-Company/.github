## How we operate

```text
Understand  →  Stabilize  →  Improve  →  Document  →  Operate
```

1. **Understand the environment.** Identify the people, systems, vendors, dependencies, and work that cannot stop.
2. **Protect what matters first.** Prioritize access, data, continuity, and the highest-consequence risks.
3. **Make controlled changes.** Define scope, explain tradeoffs, obtain approval, and implement in a sensible sequence.
4. **Verify the outcome.** Check the actual service, device, workflow, or recovery path—not just the configuration screen.
5. **Leave a useful record.** Document decisions, changes, ownership, remaining questions, and the next practical step.

## Our priorities

- **Business continuity:** Keep people productive and prepare the systems the business depends on to recover.
- **Secure, usable access:** Protect identities, devices, administrative paths, and business information without creating needless friction.
- **Clear accountability:** Define who owns the decision, the change, the vendor relationship, and the next action.
- **Supportable standards:** Reduce one-off configurations and favor technology that can be maintained, explained, and handed over.
- **Evidence over assumptions:** Separate what is configured from what is working, and verify material changes in the real environment.
- **Right-sized improvement:** Build a practical roadmap around business impact, risk, cost, and timing.
